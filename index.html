A sample day

A remote worker’s day in the mountains.

The day we’re designing for. Take what helps, skip what doesn’t.

  1. 6:30 AM

    Start early, outside.

    A slow walk in the morning air before any screen. If there’s mist, even better.

  2. 8:00–11:30 AM

    Deep work first.

    Mornings are cool and calls are fewer. Give them to your hardest task.

  3. 12:30 PM

    Lunch, then a reset.

    Eat away from the desk. Then a short walk or a 20-minute nap, with an alarm set.

  4. 2:00–5:00 PM

    Calls after lunch.

    Meetings, replies and shallow work, once the deep work is done. In the monsoon, rain often comes in the afternoon, so pick a quiet spot for calls ahead of time.

  5. 5:30 PM

    Golden hour, screen off.

    Step away at sunset. A walk or a game of badminton while there’s still light.

  6. 7:30 PM on

    Offline evening, early night.

    Dinner, a campfire, real conversation. Then sleep early: mornings are the best part.

Pack for patchy days.

Power and network can drop in the hills. Bring a power bank, a backup hotspot on a different network, offline copies of the day’s files, and earphones with a mic.

A sample day, not a rule. Adjust the times to your time zone and your team.
